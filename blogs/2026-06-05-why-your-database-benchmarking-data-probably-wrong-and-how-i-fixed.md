Hidden bottlenecks in testing environments can mask true database performance, including undersized load generators, inefficient I/O patterns, and PostgreSQL write-ahead log checkpoint operations. By systematically eliminating these confounding factors through proper provisioning, configuration adjustments, and automation, reliable benchmarking results that reflect actual database capacity rather than environmental constraints can be achieved.
