Outlines installation and upgrade pathways for Ansible Automation Platform 2.7, covering containerized deployments on RHEL and operator-based deployments on OpenShift Container Platform with growth and enterprise topology options. It distinguishes upgrades from migrations, highlights the removal of the RPM-based installer requiring existing RPM deployments to migrate to containerized methods, and provides pre-upgrade validation checklists for moving from version 2.6.
