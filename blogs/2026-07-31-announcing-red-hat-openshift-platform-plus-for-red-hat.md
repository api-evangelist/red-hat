Organizations using Red Hat OpenShift Service on AWS (ROSA) are increasingly seeking ways to extend their platform’s capabilities with enterprise-grade security and data services. Red Hat OpenShift Platform Plus, is now available for ROSA on the AWS Marketplace. By consolidating advanced multicluster management, security, and data storage into a single procurement option, we’re making it easier for you to scale your cloud operations while drawing down your committed AWS spend.
