A guide to integrating CyberArk with Red Hat Enterprise Linux Identity Management to enable automated password rotation. It introduces system accounts, a new feature in RHEL 10.2 and 9.8 that bypass standard user account constraints like mandatory password resets and OTP requirements, and walks through configuring the system account in IdM, the privilege-permission-role hierarchy, and importing the RedHatFreeIPA plugin in CyberArk.
