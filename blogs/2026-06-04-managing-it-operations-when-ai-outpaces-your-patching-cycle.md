AI has dramatically accelerated vulnerability discovery, but traditional patch cycles cannot keep pace with machine-speed threat detection, requiring organizations to shift from time-bound security routines to real-time event-driven responses. Automated orchestration is needed to bridge the gap between threat identification and remediation, containing threats in minutes rather than days while maintaining system uptime.
