Enterprise AI adoption is hitting a regulatory wall. Organizations in highly regulated sectors—from global banking to defense and life sciences—possess petabytes of valuable data. But privacy regulations, national data sovereignty mandates, and intellectual property risks prevent them from training models on central clouds or third-party AI platforms.Duality Technologies is a leader in privacy-enhancing technologies (PETs), helping organizations collaborate and derive value from sensitive data without exposing the underlying information.
