In July, the OpenClaw Foundation was announced with Red Hat joining as a sponsor to bring open, more secure infrastructure to autonomous AI agents. OpenClaw has quickly become a premier open source autonomous agent framework, and Red Hat’s involvement aims to bring the enterprise readiness and infrastructure needed to transition this technology from a powerful personal tool to a production-grade business asset. The value of OpenClaw lies in its persistence.
