Agentic AI systems shift beyond simple prompts to autonomous enterprise action, requiring organizations to avoid vendor lock-in by maintaining control over their data and choosing appropriate models for specific use cases across hybrid cloud environments. Red Hat advocates for coherence rather than centralization through open source foundations, enabling diverse agent portfolios to be built and scaled without dependency on single vendors.
