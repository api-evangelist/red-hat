Applications are moving beyond predefined workflows toward agentic systems that can reason, use tools, interact with enterprise data, execute code, delegate work and collaborate with other agents to accomplish goals. This has driven the development of new capabilities in areas like agent sandboxing to secure agent execution, AI gateways to secure access to models and tools, AgentOps for tracing, observability, evaluation and more. We believe another important layer of the enterprise AI stack is emerging: an open control plane for agents.
