The Middle East is transitioning from AI experimentation to production-ready innovation, with AI projected to contribute USD 320 billion to the region's economy by 2030, but 76% of UAE organizations report a critical AI skills gap. Organizations need flexible, open-source platforms to deploy AI models seamlessly across on-premises, public cloud, and edge environments without refactoring their technology stack for each diverse regulatory market.
