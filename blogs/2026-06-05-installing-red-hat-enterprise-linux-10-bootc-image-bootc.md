Red Hat Enterprise Linux 10 introduces a modern deployment method using bootc images through the Anaconda installer, enabling immutable system installations from container images with atomic updates and rollbacks. The new bootc kickstart command, available as a Technology Preview in RHEL 10.2, simplifies deployments compared to the older ostreecontainer command while offering consistent, reliable system management.
