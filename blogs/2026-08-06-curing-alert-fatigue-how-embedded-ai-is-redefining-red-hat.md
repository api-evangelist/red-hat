Between virtual machines, microservices, and AI pipelines, hybrid clouds can be incredibly complex and can bring an unwelcome partner: alert fatigue. SREs and IT OPs teams face a constant flood of disconnected alerts, forced to manually stitch together metrics from completely different monitoring tools. Sifting through raw logs and writing complex PromQL queries just to build static dashboards isn't sustainable.Something has to change.
